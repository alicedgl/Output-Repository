## FIX (Mandatory) -- Max 5 Per Format

### Fix 1: Blog -- Reader Respect (Style Critic) + Required Outcomes (Reality Critic)

**TARGET DIMENSION:** Reader Respect (currently 6/10) and Required Outcomes (currently 5/8)

- **Current:** Lines 81-87, "Key takeaways" section:
  ```
  ## Key takeaways

  - Nine percent of contracts auto-renew unintentionally, costing companies $1.5 million or more per year in missed termination windows alone
  - AI-native contract intelligence gives CFOs self-service access to portfolio data, replacing spreadsheets and manual tracking with 26-second AI-powered reviews
  - Forward-looking contract management means detecting risks before they hit the P&L, not cleaning up after the fact
  - Teams using AI-native CLM report 40 to 60 percent reductions in administrative time and more than 500 hours saved annually on data extraction
  - The difference between AI-native and bolt-on determines whether your finance team gets real value or just another tool to manage
  ```
- **Problem:** Every bullet restates information from the blog body verbatim. Zero new information for a reader who read the article. This is the primary driver of the Reader Respect score (6/10) and a missed opportunity for actionable guidance (Required Outcomes 5/8). The panel debate confirmed this is the single highest-leverage fix.
- **Required change:** Replace the key takeaways section with a practical evaluation framework. Title it "How to evaluate contract intelligence for your organization" or "Questions to ask your team this quarter." Provide 4-5 actionable items that a CFO can use independently without contacting Concord. Examples:
  - "Audit your current renewal tracking: How many contracts auto-renewed in the last 12 months without a deliberate decision?"
  - "Measure your contract data access time: How long does it take your team to answer a question about a specific contract's terms?"
  - "Evaluate your AI integration: Does your current platform include AI extraction on all plans, or is it a premium add-on?"
  - "Test your portfolio visibility: Can you generate a report of all contracts expiring this quarter in under five minutes?"
  - "Calculate your exposure: What is the total value of contracts that renewed in the last year without finance review?"
- **Acceptance criteria:** The new section must (a) contain zero repetition of body content, (b) provide at least 4 items the reader can act on independently, and (c) maintain the blog within the 800-1,500 word count range.

### Fix 2: Blog -- Data Integrity (Strategist)

**TARGET DIMENSION:** Data Integrity (currently 3/5)

- **Current:** Line 7: "Industry research estimates that nine percent of contracts auto-renew unintentionally due to missed deadlines, and missed termination windows cost companies $1.5 million or more annually."
- **Problem:** The blog's headline stat has no named source. "Industry research" is not a credible citation for a CFO audience. This stat anchors the entire blog and appears three times. The Strategist flagged this as a defensibility risk; all three critics confirmed the concern during debate.
- **Required change:** Add attribution context to the first appearance. Options:
  - If a specific study can be identified from the source files, name it: "According to [Study Name/Firm], nine percent of contracts..."
  - If no specific study exists, reframe with appropriate hedging: "Legal operations benchmarking data suggests that as many as nine percent of contracts auto-renew unintentionally..." or lead with the better-sourced stat and subordinate the nine percent.
  - Apply the same improvement to "71 percent of companies miss contract renewals, according to legal operations research" (line 13) -- either name the source or improve the attribution.
- **Acceptance criteria:** The three most prominent third-party statistics (nine percent, 71 percent, $1.5M) must have more specific attribution than "industry research" or "legal operations research." Named sources are ideal; improved contextual framing is the minimum.

**TARGET DIMENSION:** Writing Craft (currently 7/10)
