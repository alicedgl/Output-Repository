### What Improved from Iteration 1 to 2

1. **Evaluation framework replaced key takeaways** (+1 Required Outcomes, +1 Extra Mile Value). Five actionable self-assessment checks with zero repetition of body content.
2. **Capability subsection structural variety** (+1 Writing Craft). Four distinct patterns: standard, quote-first, question-led, stat-led.
3. **Email time-to-value proof point** (+1 Buyer Journey Fit). Implementation angle that does not appear in blog or LinkedIn.
4. **Multi-entity edge case** (+1 Competence Proof). Insider scenario about subsidiary contract conflicts.
5. **Statistical framing improvement** (0 on Data Integrity, but reduced defensibility risk). "Legal operations benchmarking data" replaces generic "industry research."
6. **"Flying blind" metaphor thread** (+1 Voice Consistency). Three callbacks create thematic coherence with the title.

### What Did Not Improve (Persistent Issues)

1. **Data Integrity (3/5):** The nine percent, 71 percent, and $1.5M stats lack named third-party sources. The source brief acknowledges this gap. Layer 2 should be tasked with identifying or commissioning citable benchmarking studies for future CFO-targeted content.
2. **Reader Respect (6/10):** Transition filler sentences remain ("The pattern is familiar," "These are not edge cases"). Stat repetition across body and FAQ persists (40-60 percent appears three times).
3. **Feeling Valued (2/4):** The blog never acknowledges the reader's existing processes or constraints. Adding one empathetic sentence would improve this score.

### Panel Consensus

All three critics agree the evaluation framework is the blog's strongest section and the highest-impact revision. The customer proof layer (eight quotes from six companies) remains the primary differentiator. The content is ready for human review with the non-blocking improvement suggestions listed below.

### Debate Highlights

- **Reader Respect and FAQ repetition:** Style Critic held 6/10 citing filler transitions; Reality/Strategy argued FAQ repetition is format-driven. Moderator ruled filler independently justifies the score.
- **Data Integrity and unnamed sources:** Style Critic argued framing improvement should earn credit; Strategist held 3/5 citing six to seven unattributed stats exceeding the one-to-two threshold for 4/5. Moderator upheld.
