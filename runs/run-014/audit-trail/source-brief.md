## Feature Details

### AI Text Extraction
- Automatically analyzes agreements and extracts key data points, clauses, and metadata
- Extracts party names, dates, financial terms, obligations
- Supports both system-defined and custom AI property extraction schemas
- Single-document and bulk extraction (up to 100 documents per job)
- Preview/dry-run mode to test extraction before committing
- Extraction transparency: see exactly where each data point was extracted from
- Processing time: approximately 10 to 30 seconds per document
- Accuracy: 90 to 98 percent for structured templates, 80 to 92 percent for semi-structured documents
- Available in Business and Enterprise plans
- **Source:** `context/context/features/ai-text-extraction.md`

### AI Assistant (Natural Language Querying)
- Chat-based interface for natural language conversations about agreements
- Ask questions in plain language: "What are the termination conditions?"
- Semantic search across the entire document library
- Upload up to 10 files for comparative analysis
- Generate new agreements from descriptions
- Context-aware: automatically understands the current document
- Available in all plans
- **Source:** `context/context/features/ai-assistant.md`

### AI Summarization
- Generates plain-language summaries highlighting key terms, obligations, and risks
- Executive, legal, operational, financial, and compliance summary types
- Reduces initial review from hours to minutes
- Guest summary access for external sharing
- **Source:** `context/context/features/ai-summarization.md`

### Negotiation (Redlining)
- Track changes with insert and delete proposals
- Multi-party collaboration with full audit trail
- Internal vs. external comments for team discussion
- Word import/export with track changes preserved
- Amendment history showing evolution of each change
- Available in all plans
- **Source:** `context/context/features/negotiation.md`

### Clauses Library
- Centralized repository of pre-written, reusable text blocks
- Insert approved contract language with a click
- Usage tracking shows how many templates reference each clause
- Reduces risk of non-standard terms in agreements
- **Source:** `context/context/features/clauses-library.md`

### Approval Workflows
- Sequential and parallel approval steps
- Conditional rules (e.g., "if contract value > $50K, require Finance approval")
- Block external signing until internal approvals complete
- Group-based validators for team flexibility
- Full audit trail of every approval decision
- **Source:** `context/context/features/approvals-v2.md`

## Competitive Differentiators

### AI-Native vs. Bolt-On AI

**Core positioning:** Concord's Agreement Intelligence is built into the platform from the ground up. Competitors have bolted AI features onto legacy contract management systems. This distinction matters for legal teams because:

1. **AI included in all plans.** Competitors like DocuSign CLM and Ironclad charge extra for AI features or require higher-tier plans. Concord includes AI text extraction, AI assistant, and AI summarization without additional licensing.

2. **26-second contract reviews.** Concord's AI processes and extracts key data from a contract in 26 seconds, compared to 92 minutes for manual review. This is a Concord platform benchmark.

3. **Extraction transparency.** Concord shows exactly where in the document each data point was extracted from. DocuSign CLM does not provide this transparency. Legal teams need to verify AI outputs, and transparency enables trust.

4. **Full lifecycle integration.** AI works across drafting, negotiation, signing, and post-execution management. Point solutions like ContractSafe (storage only) or Dropbox Sign (e-signature only) lack this end-to-end AI coverage.

### vs. Enterprise Platforms (Ironclad, Agiloft, Conga)
- **Implementation speed:** Concord in less than one day vs. Ironclad three to six months, Agiloft two to three months
- **Cost:** Concord starting at $399/month vs. Ironclad $15,000+/year
- **Usability:** Gmail-like interface vs. steep learning curves
- **Native e-signature:** Included at no extra cost vs. third-party DocuSign fees

### vs. Mid-Market CLM (Juro, ContractBook, LinkSquares)
- **Faster implementation:** Less than one day vs. weeks
- **Transparent pricing:** No surprise fees
- **Deeper AI:** Agreement Intelligence vs. basic extraction

### vs. Point Solutions (ContractSafe, ContractWorks)
- **Full CLM:** Create, negotiate, sign, and manage in one platform
- **In-app editing:** Full editing vs. view-only
- **Clause library:** Maintain and reuse approved language
